# Product

## Register

product

## Users

UTHM students, lecturers, technicians, and faculty managers use the system to access facility booking, equipment loans, maintenance reporting, and operational tools.

## Product Purpose

FSKTM FMS coordinates facility access and maintenance across the faculty. Users should reach the tools allowed by their verified account with minimal friction. Student and lecturer identities are determined by their verified UTHM Google email; staff keep a separate sign-in portal.

## Brand Personality

Clear, trustworthy, official. Keep the existing UTHM and FSKTM identity and communicate access requirements plainly.

## Anti-references

- Separate student and lecturer login portals for the same Google sign-in flow.
- A separate student or lecturer registration process.
- Password entry or demo credential autofill for student and lecturer access.
- Demo credential autofill on the staff portal.

## Design Principles

- Make the correct sign-in path obvious at a glance.
- Let verified university identity determine academic role.
- Keep staff access distinct and easy to find.
- Use familiar controls and clear feedback for authentication errors and loading states.
- Preserve UTHM identity without adding visual noise.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Keep the login flows keyboard-operable, with explicit keyboard access prioritized for staff and operations users. Use visible focus, readable contrast, semantic labels, and reduced-motion support throughout.
